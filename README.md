# DriveTelemSynth AI – Multi-Vehicle Driving Simulation and Intelligent Insurance Analytics Platform

**DriveTelemSynth AI** is an advanced full-stack driving telematics simulation, multi-vehicle behavioral benchmarking, and Usage-Based Insurance (UBI) analytics platform built with Python, Flask, NumPy, and Pandas.

It generates reproducible, mathematically coherent synthetic driving telematics for multiple vehicles traveling at diverse speeds, visualizes them in an interactive Canvas-based simulation studio, calculates heuristic driving risk scores, evaluates simulated insurance eligibility, and generates tailored safety coaching recommendations.

> **Disclaimer:** All vehicle telemetry, driver identities, risk scores, and insurance assessments generated by this software are 100% synthetic heuristics created for algorithmic testing and academic demonstration. They do not constitute actual motor vehicle underwriting, real driver evaluations, or commercial financial quotations.

## 2. Key Features

1. **Multi-Vehicle Comparative Generation:**
   - Concurrently simulates between 2 and 5 vehicles with configurable target speeds, categories (Slow, Medium, High), and driving behaviours (Safe, Moderate, Aggressive).
   - Generates unique vehicle identifiers, synthetic driver identities from `Faker`, distances, durations, speeding ratios, night driving ratios, and harsh driving event counts.

2. **Multi-Vehicle Driving Simulation Studio:**
   - HTML5 Canvas road simulation powered by `requestAnimationFrame`.
   - Simulates horizontal multi-lane asphalt with animated road markings, speed limits, vehicle bodies, headlights, taillights, and live speed tags.
   - Vehicles travel at speeds strictly proportional to their configured or simulated velocities.
   - Comprehensive controls: Start, Pause, Resume, Reset, Live Speed Sliders, and Sync with Fleet.

3. **Relative Speed Risk & Physical Consistency:**
   - Duration is mathematically derived from distance and sampled speed: $\text{Duration (min)} = \frac{\text{Distance (km)}}{\text{Speed (km/h)}} \times 60$.
   - Speeding is evaluated **relative to the configured road speed limit**, ensuring highway driving at 100 km/h on a 120 km/h road is not unfairly penalized.

4. **100-Point Risk Scoring Engine:**
   - Transparent, bounded heuristic evaluating speeding ratio ($45\%$), harsh braking ($20\%$), harsh acceleration ($20\%$), and night driving ($15\%$).
   - Classified into Low ($<35$), Medium ($35\text{--}64$), and High ($\ge 65$) risk tiers.

5. **Intelligent Insurance Eligibility Engine:**
   - Calculates illustrative Pay-How-You-Drive (PHYD) tiers:
     - **Preferred Safe Driver (Low Risk):** Green badge, eligible for 20% safe-driving discount.
     - **Standard Coverage (Medium Risk):** Amber badge, eligible for 5% baseline discount.
     - **High Risk / Surcharge Tier (High Risk):** Red badge, 0% discount, flagged for review.
   - Computes baseline and final simulated premiums (e.g., base ₹10,000 $\rightarrow$ ₹8,000 for Preferred tier).

6. **Fleet Safety Ranking & Best Driver Analysis:**
   - Automatic safety ranking (Rank 1 = Safest vehicle).
   - Explanatory justification highlighting why the safest vehicle won, distinguishing defensive driving discipline from mere slow driving.

7. **Tailored Safety Coaching Recommendations:**
   - Analyzes individual telemetry components to generate distinct, vehicle-specific recommendations (speed compliance, following distance, throttle moderation, night visibility).

8. **Dynamic Chart.js Visualizations:**
   - Average Speed by Vehicle vs. Road Limit (Bar)
   - Risk Score Comparison (Bar)
   - Simulated Insurance Premium Comparison (Bar)
   - Harsh Driving Events: Braking vs. Acceleration (Grouped Bar)
   - Fleet Risk Tier Distribution (Doughnut)
   - Multi-Axis Driving Behaviour Profile (Radar)

9. **Data Export & Privacy-Safe In-Memory Architecture:**
   - Export full multi-vehicle datasets as CSV or complete analytical JSON.
   - Thread-safe in-memory caching (`OrderedDict` with `threading.Lock()`, FIFO eviction capped at 32 datasets). Zero driver data written to disk.

## 3. Mathematical & Actuarial Formulation

### A. Physical Trip Constraints
$$\text{Duration (minutes)} = \frac{\text{Distance (km)}}{\text{Average Speed (km/h)}} \times 60.0$$
$$\text{Maximum Speed (km/h)} = \text{Average Speed} + \Delta v_{\text{offset}} \quad (\Delta v_{\text{offset}} > 0)$$

### B. Poisson Incident Distribution
Driving incidents (harsh braking, rapid acceleration) are modeled as discrete spatial Poisson processes:
$$\lambda = \text{Rate per 10 km} \times \left(\frac{\text{Distance in km}}{10.0}\right)$$
$$\text{Events} \sim \text{Poisson}(\lambda)$$

### C. 100-Point Risk Heuristic
$$\text{Points}_{\text{speeding}} = \text{Speeding Ratio} \times 45.0$$
$$\text{Points}_{\text{braking}} = \min\left(\frac{\text{Harsh Braking Count}}{\max(\text{Distance} / 10, 1)}, 1.0\right) \times 20.0$$
$$\text{Points}_{\text{acceleration}} = \min\left(\frac{\text{Harsh Acceleration Count}}{\max(\text{Distance} / 10, 1)}, 1.0\right) \times 20.0$$
$$\text{Points}_{\text{night}} = \text{Night Driving Ratio} \times 15.0$$
$$\text{Risk Score} = \left\lfloor \text{clip}\left(\sum \text{Points}, 0, 100\right) + 0.5 \right\rfloor$$

### D. Simulated Insurance Premium Calculation
$$\text{Discount Amount} = \text{Base Premium} \times \left(\frac{\text{Discount \%}}{100}\right)$$
$$\text{Final Simulated Premium} = \text{Base Premium} - \text{Discount Amount}$$

## 4. API Reference

| Method | Endpoint | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `GET` | `/` | Web application home & interactive dashboard. |
| `GET` | `/health` | Microservice health check & version info. |
| `POST` | `/api/generate-multiple` | Generates multi-vehicle telematics, comparisons, insurance, and download links. |
| `POST` | `/api/simulate` | Returns real-time simulation lane and speed parameters. |

| `POST` | `/api/insurance-assessment` | Evaluates insurance tiers and simulated premiums. |
| `GET` | `/api/vehicle/<vehicle_id>` | Retrieves specific vehicle details and trip samples from active fleet. |
| `GET` | `/api/comparison/<id>` | Retrieves cached comparison analysis. |
| `GET` | `/download/<download_id>` | Streams complete dataset as CSV attachment from memory. |
| `GET` | `/download-json/<download_id>` | Streams complete fleet analytics as JSON attachment from memory. |
| `POST` | `/api/generate` | Single-scenario legacy dataset generation route. |
